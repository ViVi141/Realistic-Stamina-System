RSS v6.1.5 Change Notes
=======================
From 6.1.4 → 6.1.5 (config 6.1.5). Breath SFX + AI stamina default.
Keep server & clients on the same mod build.

[EN]
• Breath sounds: re-enabled; Settings → RSS “Breath Sounds” is a local client
  toggle (does not sync). Less false panting on easy Run (higher audible
  threshold / excess ref; cruise band below 1.12×CP).
• AI stamina calc off by default (Disable AI Stamina). Combat effects stay off.
  Existing RealisticStaminaSystem.json is not overwritten.

[中文]
• 呼吸音：重新开启；设置 → RSS「Breath Sounds」为本机开关，不同步服务器。
  收紧假喘（可闻门限 / 超额参考；P<1.12×CP 巡航不计超额）。
• AI 体力计算默认关闭；实验性 AI 战斗效果仍默认关。
  已有 RealisticStaminaSystem.json 不会被自动改写。
